# Company FAQs

**Q: How do I return a product?**
A: Please contact support@acmecompany.com within 30 days of delivery. We'll provide a prepaid label and issue a refund once the return is received.

**Q: What are your support hours?**
A: Our customer support team is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM PST. We respond to most messages within 24 hours.

**Q: How can I track my order?**
A: After your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking link. You can also check your order status anytime by logging into your account.

**Q: Can I cancel or modify an order after it's placed?**
A: Orders can be canceled or modified within 1 hour of placement. After that, we begin processing immediately and cannot guarantee changes.

# Policy Documents

**Refund Policy**
Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. Products must be returned in their original condition and packaging. Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a company error.
